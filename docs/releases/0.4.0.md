Say what you want done, get the command. Or watch your words appear as you speak.

**Type While Speaking.** Check it in the menu and dictation appears while you talk instead of after you stop. The last few words settle as Glossa hears more; finished sentences lock and are never retyped, and only the unlocked end is redone when you stop. Click elsewhere or press a key while talking and Glossa stops typing for that recording, with the full text on the clipboard. Off by default; best in terminals and plain text fields.

**Pick your microphone.** A *Microphone* menu lists every input, plus *System Default*. A recording that comes back silent is no longer sent (Whisper used to answer silence with your glossary in a loop); a notice names the device instead.

**Fewer Whisper ghosts.** Subtitle credits ("Closed Captioning by …"), phrases repeated back to back ("All right. All right. …") and glossary echoes are dropped, and the silence before you press stop is trimmed so Whisper has nothing to fill.

**New sounds.** One family of short tones: rising on start, falling on stop, softer for dictation and brighter for commands, with distinct ones for done, check this command, a question back, and failed.

**Voice commands.** Hold Shift while you press the microphone key (or hold or double-tap Fn) and say something like "show me the five biggest files here". Glossa transcribes it, sends it to a chat model together with the frontmost app's window title, the last 30 lines on screen and this Mac's host, user and shell, and pastes one command **without Return**. You read it and press return yourself.

If the request is unclear, the model asks a short question instead; commands that delete, use `sudo`, touch disks, force-push or kill processes come with a warning. Both appear in a small notice at the top of the screen.

**Tell it about your setup.** `~/.config/glossa/setup.txt` (*Edit Setup Notes…* in the menu) is sent with each command: your servers and ssh aliases, where projects live, your shell helpers. "Restart the app on production" then becomes `ssh web1 'docker restart app-web'` instead of a question.

With an llm-router or LiteLLM `endpoint`, the chat URL is derived from it. Set `assistApiKey` if your key is scoped to transcription only, and `assistModel` (default `code`) to pick the model:

```json
{
  "endpoint": "http://100.x.y.z:7576/v1/audio/transcriptions",
  "apiKey": "sk-…transcribe-only",
  "assistApiKey": "sk-…allowed-to-use-code",
  "assistModel": "code"
}
```
